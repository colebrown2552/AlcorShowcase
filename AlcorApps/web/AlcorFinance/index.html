How it's calculated

Each amount grows once a year: amount × (1 + rate)years. The baseline column does the same at the baseline rate over that holding's own years. Difference is the baseline value minus the holding's value, and the gap is that difference as a share of the baseline value.

Totals add values reached in different years, so they don't describe one balance on one date. The average rate is weighted by amount and ignores time.

How it's calculated

Payment = amount × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n), where r is the yearly rate divided by 12 and n is the number of months. At 0% it's the amount divided by n.

The schedule uses the fixed-payment formula before rounding. Principal and interest are rounded across the schedule so displayed rows add up; individual payments may vary by a few cents. Lender schedules and rounding can differ.

How it's calculated

Margin per unit = price − cost per unit. Units to break even = fixed costs ÷ margin per unit, rounded up because you can't sell part of a unit. Break-even sales = those units × price.
